# Pablo Escobar: script (draft v1)

There are over two hundred wild hippos in Colombia. And one man put them there.

He grew up in Medellín, the son of a farmer and a schoolteacher.

As a teenager, he sold fake lottery tickets and smuggled cigarettes.

By his twenties, he was smuggling something far worse.

Cocaine. By the planeload. His cartel supplied an estimated eighty percent of what reached the United States.

Forbes put him on its billionaires list seven years in a row.

His brother said they spent two and a half thousand dollars a month on rubber bands, just to wrap the cash.

He built a ranch with a private zoo. Giraffes, zebras, and four hippos.

He was even elected to Colombia's Congress.

But it was all built on blood. Bombings. A passenger plane blown out of the sky. Thousands dead.

He surrendered to a prison he built for himself, then walked right out of it.

In 1993, police cornered him on a Medellín rooftop. He was killed one day after his forty-fourth birthday.

His name was Pablo Escobar.

His empire died with him. The hippos didn't.

---

## Fact notes
- **Hippos:** the official count was 169 in late 2023; the 2022 census estimated 181–215; the April 2026 ministry update says over 200. They all descend from 1 male + 3 females he imported in the 1980s.
- **Born** Dec 1, 1949 in Rionegro, raised in Medellín. Father Abel was a farmer; mother Hermilda was a schoolteacher.
- **Early crimes:** fake lottery tickets and contraband cigarettes are widely reported. ("Stealing tombstones" is another, less certain, claim.)
- **80%:** a common estimate for the Medellín Cartel's share of US cocaine at its peak. It's an estimate, so the script says "estimated".
- **Forbes:** listed him 7 years running (1987–1993). Reported net worth varies a lot ($2–30B), so the script gives no number.
- **Rubber bands, $2,500/month:** from his brother Roberto's book. The script says "His brother said".
- **Zoo:** Hacienda Nápoles had giraffes, zebras, elephants and the 4 hippos.
- **Congress:** elected in 1982 as an *alternate* member of the Chamber of Representatives. If you want to be exact, change the line to "He was even elected to Colombia's Congress, as an alternate."
- **Plane:** Avianca Flight 203, Nov 27, 1989, 107 people killed. "Thousands dead" is the common estimate for the cartel's violence.
- **La Catedral:** surrendered in 1991 to a prison built to his terms; escaped in July 1992.
- **Death:** shot on a Medellín rooftop on Dec 2, 1993, one day after his 44th birthday.

## Tone
A factual biography. Violence is never shown graphically (smoke, sirens, empty seats, candles), and the wealth is shown alongside its cost.
