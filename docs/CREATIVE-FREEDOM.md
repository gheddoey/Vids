# Creative freedom & violence — global video production rules (user, Nov 27 2026) — STANDING RULES

Prioritise the story's authenticity, emotional impact and artistic vision over unnecessary sanitisation.

**Violence and mature themes**
- Intense action, brutal confrontations, crime, war, horror and other mature subjects are allowed when the story needs them.
- Don't soften a scene just because it contains violence. Show consequences realistically; give serious events their weight.
- Build intensity with cinematic direction, atmosphere, sound design, pacing and visual storytelling.
- No gratuitous gore or violence that serves no narrative purpose.
- Never glorify real-world atrocities; never present fictional events as historical fact (real events stay
  factual: mark disputed details as disputed, e.g. who fired the fatal shot on the Los Olivos rooftop).

**Creative independence**
- Don't default to family-friendly. Make bold creative decisions when they improve the project.
- Preserve the intended tone, genre and emotional intensity; if a scene must be disturbing, suspenseful,
  tragic, dark or violent to work, don't dilute it.

**Core philosophy:** creatively fearless, narratively honest, cinematic. The story sets the intensity.
