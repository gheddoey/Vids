# D.B. Cooper: script v1

**Title:** *He Jumped With $200,000 and Vanished*

## Five hooks
1. "A man jumped out of a jet, at night, into a storm, with two hundred thousand dollars. He was never seen again."
2. "The only unsolved hijacking in American history started with a bourbon and soda."
3. "Nine years after he vanished, a little boy dug up his money."
4. "His name wasn't D.B. Cooper. A reporter got it wrong, and the wrong name became a legend."
5. "He left behind a clip-on tie, and a mystery the FBI couldn't crack in forty-five years."

**Pick: #1.** It's the clearest at a glance (jet, night, storm, cash), the stakes are concrete, and it sets up the central
question (where did he go?) that the whole video pays off. #2 is cleverer but needs a beat to land. #3 and #4 are
stronger as mid-video turns, so they're used there. #5 becomes the closing callback.

## Sound and music
- **Bed:** a low 70s noir pulse (Rhodes, bass, brushed snare) synthesized in `make_sfx.py`, entering at line 2.
- **Hook:** wind roar, rain on metal, beacon hum. Hard silence on "He was never seen again."
- **Tension rise** at the note; a sting on "I have a bomb"; a ticking clock under the demands; flashbulb pops and teletype in the FBI scene.
- **Climax:** jet engines whine, stair hydraulics, a giant wind gust, then near-silence with only rain.
- **Revelation:** a single piano note when the bundle surfaces; a "match" chime on the serial numbers.
- **Ending:** the music drops out, and one soft low tone lands on "clip-on tie". No outro prompt.

## Final payoff
The video opens on the question "where did he go?" and ends on its only answer: nobody knows. The last image is the
single thing he left behind, the tie he was wearing in the second shot.

## Self-critique and revision notes
- **Length:** about 72 s is long for a Short. If we cut, the first to go are line 8 (the passengers leaving, which can be shown without VO) and the second half of line 14. That gets to about 65 s.
- **The hook is long** (21 words, about 7 s). The first clause hits by 2 s, so it works, but a tighter option is "He jumped out of a jet with two hundred thousand dollars, and vanished."
- **Line 11 (the name)** is a slight detour. It stays because it explains the title the audience knows, and it's a fresh surprise.
- **Line 14** risks sounding flat. A punchier option: "How it got there is still a mystery. The other hundred and ninety-four thousand? Never found."
  (Fact: $200,000 − $5,800 = $194,200.)
- **Weakest stretch:** lines 8–9 (logistics). The visuals carry it: the stair drop is the payoff of the cold open.
