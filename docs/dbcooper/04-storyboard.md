## Design language: 1971 jet-age noir
Smoky amber cabin light vs storm teal outside. Materials: mustard/brown seat fabric, wood-grain panels,
chrome trim, wet tarmac with reflections, rain everywhere. Generic livery (white body, red tail, a thin
blue cheatline), with no airline name or logo. The 3D text follows the period: flip boards, typewriter, felt-tip note.

## Sound arc
A cold open of storm noise only → the noir bed enters at shot 3 → tension rises at the note → a hit on "bomb" → a ticking pulse under the
demands and FBI → the climax is wind and engines with no music → hollow rain → a light lift for the newsroom and the name → a thin bed for
the manhunt → a single piano note on the money find → silence before the last line, then one low tone.
