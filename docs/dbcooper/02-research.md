## Open questions to verify before final
- The exact jump time and altitude (sources say about 10,000 ft) are not in the VO, so this is a low risk.
- Whether he took the sewn-shut dummy reserve chute is widely reported but should be checked before any visual implies it.
- "He was never seen again" is accurate: he was never identified.
