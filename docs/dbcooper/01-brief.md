# D.B. Cooper: project brief

- **Format:** YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 70 s, VO by ElevenLabs "Mike".
- **Subject:** the Nov 24, 1971 hijacking of Northwest Orient Flight 305, the only unsolved air piracy in US history.
- **Central promise:** a man jumped from a jet into a storm with $200,000 and vanished. What happened, what was found, and why nobody knows.
- **Tone:** true-crime mystery, 1971 airline noir. Nobody was hurt, so the tone can be tense and a little wry. No glamorising: it was still a bomb threat against 36 passengers and 6 crew.
- **Design language (new for this video):** early-70s jet age. Teak and orange airport counters, a smoky 727 cabin with
  mustard and brown upholstery, Northwest-style red-tail livery (generic, no logo), FBI office with rotary phones,
  teletype and microfilm, a wet pine forest at night, a grey Columbia River sandbar in winter. Palette: nicotine amber,
  storm teal, sodium orange, cash green.
- **Rules applied:** the six rules, the production system, the set-design directive, the visual quality directive and the scriptwriting system.
- **Slice-open moment:** the parachute pack (or the briefcase "bomb"), cut open with one 3D label.
