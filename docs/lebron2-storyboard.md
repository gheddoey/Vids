# LeBron remake: storyboard (v2, six rules + RetroHD)

41 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Uses the existing ElevenLabs take (`public/vo/lebron.wav`, 18 lines),
**except line 7**: he was **17** on the Feb 18, 2002 cover, not 16 → re-record that one line as
"At seventeen, he was on the cover of a national sports magazine." (or keep the old take: your call).

**Look:** all six rules (`CLAUDE.md`). Characters rebuilt as ~150–300-tri box rigs with 64-px
code-painted faces/clothes; vertex snap + dither; RetroHD filter (subject crisp 1080p with outlines,
background dissolves to 270×480 dithered pixels; the split distance follows each shot's subject).
New tools: bloom on screens/lights/flash bulbs, drei contact shadows, Rapier confetti + ball physics,
pedalboard reverb on room sounds.
