# Pablo Escobar: storyboard (v1)

About 60 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Six rules, cinematic GTA‑like grade (`CineGrade`), dense sets.
Script: `docs/escobar-script.md`. **Tone: factual, never glamorous; violence is never shown
graphically** (smoke, sirens, empty seats, candles). Wealth is always shown next to its cost.

## Covers and objects
- **Real 64-px decals:** the 1987–1993 Forbes billionaires issues (shot 7).
- **Hand-crafted 3D:** the "PELIGRO" sign, the 80% map, the "200+" counter and the cash brick label.

## Music bed
Latin guitar plucks (synth) + soft percussion that builds through the money section; thins on "built on
blood"; silence before the name; boom; a single guitar note under the last line.
