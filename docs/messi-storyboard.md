# Lionel Messi Short — Storyboard

Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 60.2 s (voiceover tightened from 65.6 s; pauses held at 0.29 s,
a little longer before the reveal). Characters animate on twos, the camera on ones.
One-word captions, Inter Bold. No flat posters: anything "graphic" (prices, medals,
counts, the napkin, scoreboards) is a 3D object in the blocky style.

## Sound bed
Emotional minor-key pad that builds; drums enter at the move to Spain; a dip for the heartbreak;
full lift at the World Cup; drops out on "That kid who might never grow?" and lands with a boom
on "Lionel Messi".
